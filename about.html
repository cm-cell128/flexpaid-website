About FlexPaid

Built for trainers, by people who've done the admin.

FlexPaid started with a simple frustration: great trainers spending their evenings sending invoices, chasing bank transfers and reconciling who'd paid for what. We thought that time belonged with clients — not a spreadsheet.

So we built the tool we wished existed. One that bills clients automatically, puts the money straight into the trainer's own account, and keeps sessions and payments perfectly in sync — using the same payments infrastructure the biggest companies in the world rely on.
